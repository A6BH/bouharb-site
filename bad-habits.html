Bad Habits: Quit Tracker, for iPhone, iPad and Android

If you or someone else is in danger right now, call your local emergency number. In the app, the red SOS · Get help button shows helplines for your country, even without a subscription. Bad Habits is not an emergency service and can't give medical advice.

What it does

Bad Habits helps you quit or cut back on vaping, smoking, nicotine pouches, alcohol, sugar, caffeine, porn, gambling, weed, nail biting, skin picking, hair pulling and other drugs. It counts your days and the money you've saved, gives you tools for getting through a craving, and shows what sets your cravings off.

Subscription and free week

Bad Habits Pro starts with a free week, then costs $24.99 a year or $4.99 a month. It renews automatically unless you turn it off at least 24 hours before the period ends.

  • Cancel on iPhone — open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions → Bad Habits Pro.
  • Cancel on Android — open the Google Play app, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Bad Habits.
  • Cancelling during the free week means you won't be charged.
  • New phone or reinstalled — use the restore option on the subscription screen. Your subscription is tied to your Apple or Google Account, not to the phone.
  • Refunds — Apple and Google handle all payments, so refunds are requested from them: reportaproblem.apple.com on iPhone, or Google Play's order history on Android.

Your data and backups

There's no account. Everything you enter stays on your phone, and leaves it only if you back it up to your own iCloud or Google account, or export it yourself.

  • Moving to a new iPhone — on the old phone, Settings → Back up to iCloud now. On the new phone, tap Restore my data from iCloud on the welcome screen, or Settings → Restore from iCloud.
  • Moving to a new Android phone — Android backs the app up to your Google account by itself, once a day at most, while the phone charges on Wi-Fi. Set up the new phone with the same Google account and restore your backup, and the app's data comes back when it installs. Backup must be on in the phone's settings.
  • Without a cloud backup — Settings → Export all data makes a file you can keep in Files, Google Drive or AirDrop, or email to yourself. Import a backup reads it back.
  • Undo a mistake — Settings → Daily copies keeps the last 7 days on the phone.
  • Delete everything — delete the app. If you used iCloud backup, also delete the app's folder in iCloud Drive. On Android, your phone's Google backups are in the Google Drive app under Backups.

Full details are in the privacy policy.

Reminders

  • No reminders arriving — check that notifications are allowed for Bad Habits in your phone's settings, then set your time in the app under Settings → Check-in time. On Android, some phones also stop apps in the background to save battery; allow Bad Habits to run in the background in the phone's battery settings.
  • Reminders that fall in quiet hours (11 PM to 7 AM by default) are sent when they end.
  • Discreet mode in Settings keeps notifications and widgets from naming the habit.
  • Widgets — on Android, long-press the home screen, then Widgets → Bad Habits. To change which habit a widget shows, long-press it and choose Edit (the name differs between phones).

Blocking apps and sites

Settings → Block apps and sites blocks porn sites, betting and casino sites, apps you choose, and sites you add yourself. What's blocked stays blocked until you turn blocking off in the app. It shows when you track porn or gambling.

  • iPhone — it uses Screen Time, so iOS asks for permission the first time. Apple shows you the apps to choose from; Bad Habits never sees which ones you pick.
  • Android — it uses an accessibility service. The first time, Android opens its Accessibility settings: turn on Bad Habits blocking there, then come back. It reads only which app is open and, in browsers, the address bar. Nothing is stored or sent.

App lock

Settings → Lock with Face ID or fingerprint (on Android, Lock with face or fingerprint) locks the app after 3 minutes away. Your phone needs a face, a fingerprint or a passcode set up first. Get help always works, even when the app is locked.

A helpline number didn't work

Every helpline in the app comes from an official source. If one is wrong or no longer answers, tap Number didn't work? under it. That opens an email to me with the line filled in, and I'll fix it.
