Simple NAM Chain — Support

Guitar amp modelling for iPhone and iPad

What it does

Simple NAM Chain runs Neural Amp Modeler captures on your iPhone or iPad. It chains a noise gate, the amp model, an impulse response for the cabinet, and a three-band EQ. It also works as an Audio Unit (AUv3) inside hosts such as GarageBand and AUM.

What you need

  • An audio interface to plug your guitar into, for the best results. The built-in microphone works but is not intended for real playing.
  • Amp models in .nam format, and optionally impulse responses as .wav files. Thousands of free ones are available at tone3000.com.
  • Headphones or monitors. Playing through the device speaker while the microphone is open will cause feedback.

Getting models onto your device

Model packs usually arrive as a folder containing several captures of the same amp at different settings. The app is built around that:

  • Open the Files app and go to On My iPhone (or On My iPad) → Simple NAM Chain. You will find folders named Full Rig, Amp Head, Pedal, Outboard and IR.
  • Drag your model pack folders into whichever category fits. These folders are only for tidiness — you can load from anywhere on the device.
  • In the app, tap the folder icon, choose Choose Folder, and pick the pack. The arrows either side then step through every capture in it.
  • Choose File loads a single model that is not part of a pack.

Free trial and unlocking

The app is free to try for three days with everything unlocked. When the trial ends it stops processing audio until you unlock it, which is a single payment of $4.99 — not a subscription, and yours permanently.

If you reinstall the app, change device, or the app does not recognise a purchase you have already made, tap Restore Purchase on the unlock screen. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so nothing is lost.

Using it in GarageBand and other hosts

Add Simple NAM Chain as an Audio Unit effect on a track. Your unlock carries over automatically. Purchases can only be made in the app itself, so if you see a prompt to unlock inside a host, open the Simple NAM Chain app to buy or restore.

File browsing needs room on screen, so expand the plug-in view before picking a folder.

Common problems

  • No sound — check your interface is connected and selected, that the app has microphone permission in iOS Settings, and that a model is loaded.
  • Model will not load — the file must be a .nam capture. Some downloads arrive zipped and need unzipping in the Files app first.
  • The arrows do nothing — they step through the folder you chose. If you loaded a single file rather than a folder, there is nothing to step through.
