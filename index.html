How It Works

From any song to your pedalboard in seconds.

1

Pick a Song

Paste a YouTube link or type the song name. Our AI finds and isolates the guitar track.

2

AI Analyzes Tone

Advanced AI identifies the amp, cabinet, effects, and every parameter used in the song.

3

Send via Bluetooth

Connect to your GP-50 or GP-5 and send the tone directly. Your device is configured instantly.

4

Play

Start playing with the exact tone from the song. Tweak any parameter to make it your own.
