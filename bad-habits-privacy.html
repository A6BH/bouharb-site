Privacy Policy

Bad Habits: Quit Tracker

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Summary

I make money from subscriptions, not your data. Bad Habits has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and I run no servers for it. Your habits, cravings, slips, notes and reasons are stored on your phone. They leave it only if you back them up to your own iCloud or Google account, or export them yourself.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter: the habits you track, your start dates, cravings and how strong they were, triggers, slips, check-ins and moods, notes, your reasons and plans, the name and number you save for "My person", and your settings. None of it is sent to me or to anyone else.

Backups

  • iCloud backup (iPhone). The app saves a copy of your data to the app's own folder in your iCloud Drive, so you can restore it on a new phone. It is stored in your Apple Account under Apple's iCloud terms. I cannot read it.
  • Your phone's own backup (iPhone). If iCloud Backup is on in your phone's settings, Apple's backup of your phone also includes the app's data.
  • Google backup (Android). If Backup is on in your phone's settings, Android copies the app's data to your Google account, so it comes back on a new phone or when you reinstall. It is stored in your Google Account under Google's terms. I cannot read it.
  • Daily copies. The last 7 days' copies are kept on the phone only.
  • Export. When you choose Export, the app makes a file and you decide where it goes (Files, AirDrop, Google Drive, email). After that, it is in your hands.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are sold by Apple through the App Store on iPhone, and by Google through Google Play on Android. They handle payment; I never see your name, Apple or Google Account, or card details.

The app uses RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. To do that, RevenueCat receives:

  • a random ID the app makes for your install, not linked to your name or email;
  • your purchase history in this app (which plan, when it started and renews, trial status, price and store country);
  • basic technical details such as the app version, phone model, iOS or Android version and your IP address, as any internet connection does;
  • on iPhone only, Apple's ad attribution token, which tells me only whether the app was installed from an Apple Search Ads ad. It is not used to follow you across other apps or websites.

RevenueCat never receives anything about your habits. Along with subscription information, the app downloads corrections to its list of helpline numbers; this sends nothing extra.

Camera, microphone and Face ID

  • Camera. The Mirror tool shows your face live during a craving. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent.
  • Microphone. Never used. No sound is recorded.
  • Face ID, face unlock or fingerprint. If you turn on the lock, your phone checks your face or fingerprint and tells the app only whether it matched. The app never sees your face or fingerprint.

Calls and messages

"Text my person", "Call" and the helpline buttons open your phone's dialler, Messages or WhatsApp with the number filled in. The call or message is then between you and them; the app does not see it.

Blocking apps and sites

On iPhone, blocking uses Apple's Screen Time. Apple does not tell the app which apps or sites you choose; the app only holds Apple's coded references to them, on your phone.

On Android, blocking uses an accessibility service that you turn on yourself. While blocking is on, it reads only which app is open and, in browsers, the address bar, to close what you chose to block. It stores the list of apps and sites you chose on your phone. Nothing it reads is stored or sent anywhere.

Notifications, widgets and the lock screen

Reminders are scheduled on the phone, not sent from a server. Widgets and the Wait it out timer show on your home and lock screens; anyone who can see your phone can see them. Discreet mode keeps the habit's name off notifications, widgets and the lock screen.

Your country

To show helplines for your country, the app reads, on the phone, your SIM or mobile network's country (Android) or your phone's region setting. You can change it in Settings. It is not sent anywhere.

Deleting your data

  • Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on the phone.
  • To delete the iCloud backup: on your iPhone, Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage (or Storage) → Bad Habits.
  • To delete the Google backup: your phone's backups are listed in the Google Drive app under Backups, where you can delete them. Turning off Backup on the phone stops new copies.
  • To ask RevenueCat to delete your purchase records, email me and I will find and delete them with you.

Children

Bad Habits is meant for adults and is not directed at children.
